# Code of Conduct

We want participation in up-redis to be safe, respectful, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions made in the project's best interest.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and other
personally hostile conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **hello@benjsmin.com**. Reports will be
reviewed in confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants
whose behavior is harmful to the community.

This policy applies in repository discussions and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
